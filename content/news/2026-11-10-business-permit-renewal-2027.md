The Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO) announces that renewal of business permits for 2027 will run from **January 2 to 20, 2027**. Renewals after January 20 will be charged a 25% surcharge plus 2% interest per month, as provided in the Local Government Code.

## Prepare these requirements

- 2026 Mayor's permit
- 2027 barangay business clearance
- Sworn statement of 2026 gross sales or receipts
- Community Tax Certificate (cedula)

See the full guide: [Renew Business Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes](/services/business/renew-permits-and-pay-local-business-taxes).
