The municipal government is now accepting applications for its **scholarship and educational assistance program** for the second semester.

## Who can apply

Residents of Santo Niño who are enrolled in college or a technical-vocational course and who meet the grade and income requirements.

## How to apply

Submit the application form and requirements to the Office of the Mayor on or before **[deadline]**. See [Apply for the Municipal Scholarship Program](/services/education/apply-for-local-scholarships) for the complete list of requirements.
