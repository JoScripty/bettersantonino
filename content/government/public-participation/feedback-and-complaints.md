## What to include

- Your name and contact details (you may ask that these be kept confidential)
- The office or service involved
- What happened, with the date and time
- Any reference number, receipt, or photo

## National channels

- **Contact Center ng Bayan** — call 1-6565, text 0908-881-6565, or visit [contactcenterngbayan.gov.ph](https://contactcenterngbayan.gov.ph)
- **Anti-Red Tape Authority (ARTA)** — for complaints about delays or red tape in government transactions
- **eFOI** — to request government information, see [foi.gov.ph](https://www.foi.gov.ph)

Under the Ease of Doing Business Act (RA 11032), every office must act on simple transactions within 3 working days, complex ones within 7, and highly technical ones within 20.
