# Public Consultations

The Sangguniang Bayan and municipal offices hold public hearings and consultations before adopting major ordinances, the annual budget, and projects that affect communities. Everyone is welcome to attend and speak.

## How to take part

1. Attend in person at the venue and time listed.
2. Or send a written position paper to the organizing office before the hearing.
3. Bring a valid ID if you will speak during the open forum.

## Local special bodies

Civil society organizations can apply for accreditation to sit in the Municipal Development Council and other local special bodies. Ask the MPDO for the accreditation requirements.
