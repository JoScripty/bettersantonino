# Go to the Hospital for Treatment or Confinement

For cases that need a hospital, the RHU will refer you to the nearest government hospital. Emergencies should go directly to the nearest hospital emergency room.

## Who can apply

- Patients referred by the RHU
- Anyone needing emergency care

## Requirements

- Referral slip from the RHU (for non-emergency cases)
- Valid ID
- PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record
- For financial help: certificate of indigency from the barangay and MSWDO

## How to apply

1. For emergencies, call **911** or the MDRRMO hotline for ambulance transport.
2. For non-emergency cases, consult the RHU first and get a referral slip.
3. Present your referral and PhilHealth details at the hospital admitting section.
4. Ask the hospital Malasakit Center for help with remaining bills.
