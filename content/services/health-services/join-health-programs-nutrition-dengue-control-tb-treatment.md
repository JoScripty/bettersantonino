## Who can apply

- All residents
- Children identified as underweight or stunted during Operation Timbang
- Persons with TB symptoms (cough for 2 weeks or more)

## Requirements

- Barangay certificate or valid ID
- For TB: sputum sample as instructed by the RHU

## How to apply

1. Ask your Barangay Health Worker or Barangay Nutrition Scholar about upcoming activities.
2. Join Operation Timbang and supplementary feeding for children.
3. Take part in "4S" against dengue: Search and destroy breeding sites, Self-protection, Seek early consultation, Support fogging only during outbreaks.
4. If you have a lingering cough, get screened at the RHU. TB treatment is free.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
