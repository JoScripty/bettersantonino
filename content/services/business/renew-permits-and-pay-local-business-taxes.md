## Who can apply

- All businesses with an existing permit in Santo Niño

## Requirements

- Previous year's Mayor's permit
- Barangay business clearance for the current year
- Statement of gross sales or receipts for the previous year (sworn declaration or ITR)
- Community Tax Certificate (cedula)
- Updated fire safety inspection certificate

## How to apply

1. Get your barangay business clearance.
2. Submit the renewal form and requirements at the BPLO.
3. Have your taxes and fees assessed based on gross sales.
4. Pay at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (annually, semi-annually, or quarterly).
5. Claim your renewed permit.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
