## Who can apply

- MSMEs with a valid Mayor's permit
- Farmer and fisherfolk associations
- Producers of One Town One Product (OTOP) items

## Requirements

- Mayor's permit or DTI registration
- Product samples and price list
- Application form for the specific event

## How to apply

1. Watch for calls for exhibitors on the municipal Facebook page.
2. Coordinate with the municipal tourism or business development office and DTI Cagayan.
3. Submit your application before the deadline.
4. Attend the exhibitors' orientation.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
