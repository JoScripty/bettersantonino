# Rent a Stall in the Public Market

The municipality leases stalls in the Santo Niño public market to vendors.

## Who can apply

- Residents and businesses wishing to sell in the public market

## Requirements

- Letter of intent or application form
- Valid ID
- Barangay clearance
- Health certificate (for food vendors)
- Community Tax Certificate

## How to apply

1. Ask the Market Supervisor about available stalls.
2. Submit your application and requirements.
3. Wait for the award of the stall (by raffle or evaluation when there are several applicants).
4. Sign the lease contract and pay the rental and deposit.
5. Secure a Mayor's permit for your market business.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
