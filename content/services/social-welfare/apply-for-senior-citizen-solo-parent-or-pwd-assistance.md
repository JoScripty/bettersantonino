# Apply for Senior Citizen, Solo Parent, or PWD IDs and Benefits

The MSWDO and the Office of Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA) issue IDs that give discounts and benefits under national law.

## Who can apply

- Senior citizens: residents 60 years old and above (RA 9994)
- Solo parents (RA 11861)
- Persons with disability (RA 10754)

## Requirements

- **Senior citizen:** PSA birth certificate or any valid ID showing date of birth, barangay certificate of residency, 1x1 photo
- **Solo parent:** barangay certificate of solo parent status, PSA birth certificates of children, proof of solo parent circumstance (e.g. death certificate of spouse, court decision)
- **PWD:** medical certificate or certificate of disability from a licensed physician, barangay certificate of residency, 1x1 photos

## How to apply

1. Get a barangay certificate of residency.
2. Go to the MSWDO (or OSCA for seniors) and fill out the application form.
3. Submit the requirements and have your photo taken if required.
4. Claim your ID on the date given.

## Benefits

- 20% discount and VAT exemption for seniors and PWDs on medicines, medical services, transport, and restaurants
- Social pension for indigent seniors (DSWD)
- 10% discount and VAT exemption on certain items for qualified solo parents, plus monthly cash subsidy for those earning minimum wage or less
