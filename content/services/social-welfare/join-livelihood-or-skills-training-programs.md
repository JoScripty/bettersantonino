# Join Livelihood and Skills Training Programs

The municipality, with TESDA, DOLE, DSWD, and DTI, offers skills training and livelihood starter kits.

## Who can apply

- Unemployed and underemployed residents
- Out-of-school youth
- 4Ps beneficiaries and other target sectors

## Requirements

- Valid ID
- Barangay certificate
- Certificate of indigency (for livelihood assistance)

## How to apply

1. Ask the PESO or MSWDO about upcoming trainings.
2. Fill out the application or registration form.
3. Attend the training and complete the requirements.
4. For livelihood kits, prepare a simple business plan if required.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
