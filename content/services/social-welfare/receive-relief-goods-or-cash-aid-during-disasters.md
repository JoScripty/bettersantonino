# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters

Families affected by typhoons, floods, fires, and other disasters receive family food packs and other assistance from the municipality and DSWD.

## Who can apply

- Families listed in the disaster-affected list validated by the MSWDO and the barangay

## Requirements

- Family Access Card for Emergencies and Disasters (DAFAC), if issued
- Valid ID of the household head

## How to apply

1. Register as an affected family with your barangay or at the evacuation center.
2. Wait for the validation of the MSWDO.
3. Receive relief at the distribution point announced by the barangay or MSWDO.
4. For burned or totally damaged houses, ask the MSWDO about Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situation (AICS) and Emergency Shelter Assistance.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
