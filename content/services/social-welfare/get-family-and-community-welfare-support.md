# Get Family and Community Welfare Support

The MSWDO helps individuals and families in crisis, including medical, burial, transportation, and educational assistance (AICS), and handles cases of women and children in need of protection.

## Who can apply

- Individuals and families in crisis situations
- Women and children who are victims of abuse (RA 9262, RA 7610)
- Children in conflict with the law

## Requirements

- Valid ID
- Certificate of indigency from the barangay
- **Medical:** medical certificate, prescription, or hospital bill
- **Burial:** death certificate and funeral contract

## How to apply

1. Go to the MSWDO and tell the social worker what you need.
2. Undergo a short interview (case assessment).
3. Submit the requirements.
4. Receive the assistance or a referral to the appropriate agency.

## Violence against women and children

Report to the barangay VAW Desk, the PNP Women and Children Protection Desk, or call **911**. Help is confidential.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
