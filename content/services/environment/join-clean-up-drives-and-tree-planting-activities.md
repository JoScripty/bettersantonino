# Join Clean-up Drives and Tree Planting

Volunteer for river clean-ups, coastal and roadside clean-ups, and tree planting activities organized by the municipality and partner agencies.

## Who can apply

- Residents, students, organizations, and companies

## Requirements

- Registration with the organizer
- Waiver or parental consent for minors, if required

## How to apply

1. Watch for announcements on the municipal Facebook page.
2. Register your group with MENRO.
3. Bring water, a hat, gloves, and a reusable bag on the activity day.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
