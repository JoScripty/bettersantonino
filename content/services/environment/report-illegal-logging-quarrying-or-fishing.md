# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying, or Fishing

Report environmental crimes such as cutting trees without a permit, unauthorized sand and gravel extraction, and electro- or dynamite fishing.

## Who can apply

- Any resident

## Requirements

- Location and description of the activity
- Photos, plate numbers, or names if known and safe to get

## How to apply

1. Report to MENRO, the PNP, or the DENR CENRO.
2. Do not confront the violators yourself.
3. Your identity will be kept confidential.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
