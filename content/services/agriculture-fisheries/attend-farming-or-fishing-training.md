# Attend Farming or Fishing Training

The MAO, the Agricultural Training Institute (ATI), and DA hold trainings and Farmer Field Schools on rice and corn production, organic farming, and livestock raising.

## Who can apply

- RSBSA-registered farmers and fisherfolk
- Members of farmer associations and cooperatives
- Rural youth interested in agriculture

## Requirements

- RSBSA registration
- Valid ID

## How to apply

1. Ask your agricultural technician about upcoming trainings.
2. Sign up with the MAO.
3. Attend all sessions to receive your certificate.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
