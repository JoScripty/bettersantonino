# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, or Fingerlings

The Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) distributes inputs from the municipality, the Department of Agriculture (DA), and the Province of Cagayan.

## Who can apply

- Farmers and fisherfolk registered in the Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA)

## Requirements

- RSBSA enrollment (register at the MAO if not yet enrolled)
- Valid ID
- Proof of farm (tax declaration, tenancy agreement, or barangay certification)

## How to apply

1. Register in the RSBSA through the MAO or your agricultural technician.
2. Listen for announcements of distribution schedules in your barangay.
3. Claim your inputs at the distribution point and sign the acknowledgment receipt.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
