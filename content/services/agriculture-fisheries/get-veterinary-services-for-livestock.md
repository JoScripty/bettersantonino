# Get Veterinary Services for Livestock and Pets

The municipality provides vaccination, deworming, and basic treatment for livestock and poultry, and anti-rabies vaccination for dogs and cats.

## Who can apply

- Livestock and poultry raisers
- Pet owners

## Requirements

- Valid ID
- Information on the animal (type, age, number of heads)

## How to apply

1. Ask your agricultural technician or the MAO for the vaccination schedule in your barangay.
2. Bring your animals to the vaccination site, or request a farm visit for large animals.
3. For livestock transport, get a veterinary health certificate and shipping permit.

## Disease reporting

Report sudden deaths of pigs or poultry to the MAO immediately to help prevent the spread of African Swine Fever and bird flu.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
