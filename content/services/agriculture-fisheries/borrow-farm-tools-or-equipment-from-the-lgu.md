## Who can apply

- Accredited farmer associations and cooperatives
- Individual RSBSA-registered farmers (subject to guidelines)

## Requirements

- Letter of request
- RSBSA registration
- Schedule and location of farm work

## How to apply

1. Submit a letter of request to the MAO.
2. Wait for the schedule of the equipment.
3. Pay the user fee (for fuel and operator), if any.
4. Sign the equipment use agreement.

## Crop insurance

Protect your crops against typhoons and floods. Enroll with the **Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC)** through the MAO. Premiums are free for RSBSA-registered subsistence farmers.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
