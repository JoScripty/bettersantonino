## Who can apply

- All residents, especially those in flood- and landslide-prone areas

## How to apply

1. Find your evacuation center in the table below.
2. When a pre-emptive or forced evacuation is announced, leave immediately and follow your barangay officials.
3. Bring your go-bag and turn off electricity before leaving.
4. Register at the evacuation center so your family can be counted for relief.
