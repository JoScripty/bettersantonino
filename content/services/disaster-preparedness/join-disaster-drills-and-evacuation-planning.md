# Join Disaster Drills and Evacuation Planning

The MDRRMO trains barangays, schools, and families to respond to typhoons, floods, earthquakes, and fires.

## Who can apply

- Barangay officials and BDRRM committees
- Schools and offices
- Families and volunteers

## Requirements

- Letter of request (for trainings in your barangay or organization)

## How to apply

1. Join the quarterly Nationwide Simultaneous Earthquake Drill.
2. Ask the MDRRMO for training in first aid, basic life support, and water search and rescue.
3. Prepare a family emergency plan and a go-bag.

## Go-bag checklist

Water, ready-to-eat food for 3 days, flashlight and batteries, power bank, first aid kit, medicines, copies of IDs and documents in a waterproof bag, whistle, extra clothes, cash.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
