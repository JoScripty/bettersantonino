## Who can apply

- Any resident or motorist

## Requirements

- Exact location (barangay, landmark)
- Photo of the damage, if possible

## How to apply

1. Report to your barangay or directly to the Municipal Engineering Office.
2. Give the location and a description; attach photos if you can.
3. For danger to life (e.g. collapsed bridge), call the MDRRMO hotline right away.
4. The office will inspect and schedule repairs. National roads are referred to the DPWH.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
