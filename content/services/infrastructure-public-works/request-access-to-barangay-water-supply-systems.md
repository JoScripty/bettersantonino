## Who can apply

- Households in barangays served by a water system

## Requirements

- Application form
- Valid ID
- Proof of ownership or permission of the lot owner
- Barangay clearance

## How to apply

1. Apply with the water system operator (Municipal Waterworks or the barangay water association).
2. Have the site inspected.
3. Pay the connection fee and buy materials if required.
4. Wait for the installation of your connection and meter.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
