Residents and groups can reserve the municipal gymnasium, covered courts, and multipurpose halls for events.

## Who can apply

- Residents, schools, organizations, and private groups

## Requirements

- Letter of request stating the date, time, and purpose
- Valid ID of the contact person

## How to apply

1. Send a letter of request to the Office of the Mayor or the General Services Office at least one week ahead.
2. Wait for confirmation of the schedule.
3. Pay the rental fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office, if applicable.
4. Clean the facility after use.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
