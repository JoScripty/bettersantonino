## Who can apply

- Families of deceased residents of Santo Niño

## Requirements

- Death certificate (registered with the Municipal Civil Registrar)
- Valid ID of the applicant
- Burial permit

## How to apply

1. Register the death at the Municipal Civil Registrar.
2. Get a burial permit from the RHU and Municipal Civil Registrar.
3. Apply for a lot or niche at the office managing the cemetery.
4. Pay the fees at the Municipal Treasurer's Office.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
