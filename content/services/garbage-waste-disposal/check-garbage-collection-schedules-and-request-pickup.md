## Who can apply

- All households and establishments

## Requirements

- Segregated waste, tied and placed at the pickup point

## How to apply

1. Check the schedule for your barangay below.
2. Put out your segregated waste on the collection day only.
3. For bulky waste or missed collections, call MENRO.
