## Who can apply

- All households, schools, offices, and establishments

## How to apply

1. **Biodegradable** (green): food scraps, leaves, garden waste — compost at home.
2. **Recyclable** (blue): clean plastic bottles, cans, paper, cartons — sell or give to junk shops or the Materials Recovery Facility (MRF).
3. **Residual** (black): sachets, diapers, styrofoam — for collection.
4. **Special / hazardous** (red): batteries, bulbs, paint, electronics — see special waste collection.

## Do not burn garbage

Open burning is prohibited under RA 9003 and the Clean Air Act and is punishable by fines.
