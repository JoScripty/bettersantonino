## Who can apply

- Any resident

## Requirements

- Location, date, and time
- Photo or video, if safe to take

## How to apply

1. Report to your barangay or to MENRO.
2. Give the details and evidence.
3. MENRO and the barangay will inspect and issue a citation to violators.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
