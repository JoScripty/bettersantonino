Batteries, busted bulbs, paint, chemicals, used oil, and broken electronics must not be mixed with regular garbage.

## Who can apply

- All households and establishments

## Requirements

- Waste placed in a sealed, labeled container

## How to apply

1. Keep hazardous waste separate, sealed, and away from children.
2. Contact MENRO for the schedule of special waste collection or drop-off.
3. Health care waste from clinics must be handled by an accredited treater.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
