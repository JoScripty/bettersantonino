# Enroll Children in Child Development Centers (Day Care)

Barangay Child Development Centers, supervised by the MSWDO, provide early childhood care and education for children 3 to 4 years old.

## Who can apply

- Children aged 3 to 4 years old residing in Santo Niño

## Requirements

- PSA birth certificate (photocopy)
- Immunization card
- Barangay certificate of residency
- 2 pieces 1x1 ID photos (TODO: confirm)

## How to apply

1. Visit the Child Development Center in your barangay during the enrollment period (usually before the school year opens).
2. Fill out the enrollment form with the Child Development Worker.
3. Submit the requirements.
4. Attend the parents' orientation.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
