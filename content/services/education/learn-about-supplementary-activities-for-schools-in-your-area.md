# Learn About Supplementary Activities for Schools

Learners can join activities outside regular classes, such as sports meets, reading programs, contests, and youth leadership trainings.

## Who can apply

- Learners in Santo Niño public and private schools
- Out-of-school youth

## Requirements

- Parental consent form, if required by the activity

## How to apply

1. Ask the school's activity coordinator about upcoming events.
2. Follow the Sangguniang Kabataan and the municipal Facebook page for youth programs.
3. Register with your school or SK before the deadline.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
