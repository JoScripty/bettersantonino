## Who can apply

- Residents of Santo Niño enrolled or enrolling in college or technical-vocational courses
- Students who meet the grade and income requirements in the program guidelines

## Requirements

- Accomplished application form
- Certificate of registration or enrollment
- Latest report card or transcript of records
- Certificate of indigency or income tax return of parents
- Barangay certificate of residency
- Valid school ID

## How to apply

1. Watch for the call for applications on the municipal Facebook page and in [News & Updates](/news).
2. Get the application form from the Office of the Mayor (TODO: confirm office).
3. Submit the form and requirements before the deadline.
4. Attend the screening or interview if shortlisted.
5. Check the list of qualified scholars posted by the office.

## National scholarships you can also apply for

- **CHED** Tulong Dunong and merit scholarships
- **TESDA** Training for Work Scholarship Program (TWSP)
- **DOST-SEI** undergraduate science scholarships
- **UniFAST** Tertiary Education Subsidy for students in state universities and colleges

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
