## Who can apply

- Public school learners and teachers in Santo Niño
- Out-of-school youth (Alternative Learning System)
- Students who qualify for SPES summer jobs

## Requirements

- Depends on the program; see each item below

## How to apply

1. Ask your school principal about SEF-funded support such as school supplies and repairs.
2. Out-of-school youth and adults can enroll in the Alternative Learning System (ALS) through the DepEd district office.
3. Students 15–30 years old can apply for the Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES) at the PESO.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
