# Ask About Relocation Sites

Families affected by government projects or living in danger zones are entitled to relocation and assistance under the Urban Development and Housing Act (RA 7279).

## Who can apply

- Families affected by government infrastructure projects
- Families in identified danger zones

## Requirements

- Barangay certificate of residency
- Proof of being listed in the census of affected families

## How to apply

1. Ask the MSWDO or the MPDO about the list of affected families and the available relocation sites.
2. Attend the consultation required before any relocation.
3. Coordinate with your barangay for the schedule.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
