# Apply for Socialized Housing

Low-income families without homes may qualify for socialized housing programs of the municipality and national agencies (Pambansang Pabahay para sa Pilipino, NHA).

## Who can apply

- Low-income families who do not own a house or lot
- Families living in danger zones

## Requirements

- Application form
- Valid IDs of the household head and spouse
- Certificate of no property from the Municipal Assessor
- Proof of income or certificate of indigency
- Barangay certificate of residency

## How to apply

1. Inquire at the MSWDO or the municipal housing focal person about current projects.
2. Fill out the application and submit the requirements.
3. Wait for the validation and census.
4. Attend the orientation if qualified.

## Need help?

Visit the office at the Municipal Hall, Poblacion, Santo Niño, Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM, or see [Municipal Offices](/government/departments/municipal-offices) for contact numbers.
