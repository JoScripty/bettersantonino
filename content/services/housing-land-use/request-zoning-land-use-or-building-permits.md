# Get a Building Permit and Zoning Clearance

You need a locational (zoning) clearance and a building permit before you build, renovate, or demolish a structure (National Building Code, PD 1096).

## Who can apply

- Property owners and contractors

## Requirements

- Locational clearance application (MPDO)
- Proof of ownership (TCT or tax declaration) or authority from the owner
- Plans and specifications signed and sealed by licensed professionals
- Bill of materials and cost estimate
- Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance (BFP)
- Real property tax clearance

## How to apply

1. Apply for a locational clearance at the MPDO.
2. Submit the building permit application with all requirements to the Municipal Engineering Office (Office of the Building Official).
3. Pay the assessed fees.
4. Claim your building permit before starting construction.
5. Get a Certificate of Occupancy after completion.
